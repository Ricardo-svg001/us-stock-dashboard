## The Nature of Markets
The market is never wrong — only people are.
Wall Street never changes, because human nature never changes.
What happens in the market today has happened before, and will happen again.

## Trend Is Truth
Only trade when the market has a clear trend.
Buy stocks that are rising; sell stocks that are falling.
Follow the line of least resistance.

## The Value of Waiting
It was never my thinking that made the big money — it was my sitting and waiting.
Big moves take time to develop.
Not every swing is worth taking.

## Wait for Confirmation
Do not predict. Wait for the market to give you the signal.
Do not trust your own opinion until the market confirms it.
The market always gives you enough time — you never need the first second.

## Never Average Down
Never average down on a loss.
If your first trade is a loss, do not rush into the second.
Profits take care of themselves; losses never do.

## Cut Losses
What really hurts you is not the loss — it is refusing to cut it.
Decide where you will exit before you enter.
If the move does not go as expected, get out.

## Let Winners Run
Positions that are profitable from the start are where the real money comes from.
As long as the stock behaves normally, do not rush to take profits.
The big money is made by sitting still.

## Trading Frequency
Do not trade every day.
Wanting to trade every day is a major reason people lose money on Wall Street.
Much of the time, I am simply out of the market.

## Emotional Control
An investor's greatest enemy is himself.
Emotional control is the single most important skill in trading.
Hope, fear and greed are the market's greatest enemies.

## Leading Stocks
If you cannot make money in the strongest stocks, you cannot make money in the market at all.
Today's leaders may not be the leaders two years from now.
