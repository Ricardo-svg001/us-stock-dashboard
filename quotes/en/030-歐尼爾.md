## CAN SLIM
Charts plus earnings help you identify the genuinely strong stocks.
Screen on quarterly earnings, annual earnings, new products, supply and demand, leadership, institutional sponsorship, and market direction.

## Buy Breakouts Only
I only buy at the pivot buy point.
If price is more than 5% above the pivot, do not chase it.

## Cut Losses Fast
The secret to success is not being right every time — it is losing the least when you are wrong.
Any stock that falls 7–8% below your buy price gets sold. No exceptions.
Your first loss is your smallest loss.

## Study History
Studying the leading stocks of the past is the fastest way to find the next big winner.

## Selling Matters
Most investors buy better than they sell.
Sell your worst stocks first, and hold your best ones longer.
