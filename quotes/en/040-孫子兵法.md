## Win First, Then Fight
The victorious army wins first and then seeks battle — put the odds on your side before you enter.
The skilled warrior first makes himself invincible — protect capital first, pursue profit second.
First make yourself unbeatable, then wait for the enemy's opening — build risk control, then let the market bring the opportunity to you.

## Know Yourself and the Enemy
Know the other and know yourself, and you will not be endangered — understand the market, and your own weaknesses.
War is the way of deception — markets are full of false breakouts and false good news. Trust price, volume and the market itself, not the headlines.
Water has no constant shape, war no constant form — your strategy must adapt to the market cycle.

## Go With the Momentum
Seek victory in momentum, not in blaming men — trade with the trend, never against it.

## Choose Your Battlefield
Move only when there is advantage — no edge, no trade.
Avoid the strong, strike the weak — take the path of least resistance.
The skilled warrior wins where winning is easy — focus on high-probability setups, not the hardest fights.

## Discipline and Restraint
The highest form of warfare is to defeat strategy — rely on a system and discipline, not inspiration or tips.
I have heard of clumsy haste in war, but never of clever delay — trading is not constant trading. The great moves may come only a few times a year; do not waste your forces, wait for the best moment.
